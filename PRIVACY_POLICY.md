# Privacy Policy for Drink Compass

**Effective date:** September 19, 2026  
**Last updated:** September 19, 2026

Drink Compass uses your location to find nearby places and point you toward a selected destination. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

## Information used by the app

### Location and heading

With your permission, Drink Compass accesses your approximate or precise location while the app is in use. It also uses compass-heading data when available. This information is needed to:

- search for nearby places;
- compare estimated driving times;
- calculate the direction to a destination; and
- refresh results after your location changes significantly.

You can deny or revoke location permission in your device settings. The app's core search and compass features will not work without location access.

### Search and destination information

Drink Compass processes the category you choose, nearby-place search results, route estimates, and the destination you select. These details are used to provide the app's features.

## How information is processed

The developer of Drink Compass does not operate a server that collects or stores your location, heading, searches, or selected destinations. The app does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking software.

Drink Compass uses Apple services and frameworks, including Core Location, MapKit, and Apple Maps. Information required to perform place searches, retrieve route estimates, or open a business in Apple Maps may be processed by Apple under Apple's own terms and privacy policy. Apple's handling of that information is outside the developer's control.

## Data sharing

The developer does not sell your personal information and does not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Information is transmitted to Apple only as needed to provide Apple-powered location, map, search, and routing features.

## Data retention

The developer does not retain personal information from your use of Drink Compass. The app keeps limited state on your device, such as your selected place category, so it can preserve your preference between launches. You can remove this local data by deleting the app.

Apple may retain information processed through its services according to its own policies.

## Children's privacy

Drink Compass is not designed to collect personal information from children. Because the developer does not collect personal information through the app, the developer does not knowingly retain information from children.

## Security

Drink Compass relies on Apple's platform security and permission controls. No method of electronic processing is completely secure, but the app limits its use of information to what is needed for its features.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The updated policy will include a revised “Last updated” date.
